Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Quirkade ("we", "our", or "the app") is published by Buzzz Creations LLC. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Quirkade does not directly collect personal information and does not require user accounts or registration. The advertising provider described below may process limited device and advertising data.

Data Storage

Game data such as scores, preferences, and game progress is stored locally on your device. Quirkade does not operate an account or gameplay-data server. Optional WiFi multiplayer communicates only with devices participating on the same local network.

Third-Party Services

Quirkade uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location derived from an IP address to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize advertisements where permitted. Quirkade requests consent where required before requesting an advertisement. You can decline a rewarded ad and continue using the games.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children's Privacy

Quirkade is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Permissions

Quirkade does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. Local-network connectivity is used only when you choose a WiFi multiplayer mode.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
